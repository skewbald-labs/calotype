Acceptable use policy

Calotype makes images of public web pages. These are the things it may not be used for, and what happens when it is.

Last changed 20 November 2026. This policy is part of the terms of service. It applies to the free tries on the home page just as to calls made with a key.

Do not use Calotype to

  • capture, host or spread material that is illegal where you or the site are, including any sexual material involving minors;
  • build, test or spread phishing pages, malware or scams, or capture a page to pass it off as someone else's;
  • capture pages you are not allowed to view, or get around a site's login, paywall or other access controls;
  • use the images in a way that breaks someone else's rights, such as their copyright, trademark or privacy;
  • attack or probe other people's systems, or try to reach Calotype's own network or the private addresses it refuses;
  • flood a site with captures it has not agreed to, at a rate that could harm it;
  • make many free keys to get past the free plan's limit, or otherwise work around a quota or rate limit, including the limits on free tries;
  • disrupt the service for others, for example by probing for weaknesses or overloading it on purpose.

Calotype refuses private, local and cloud-metadata addresses by itself; see what Calotype will not fetch. Trying to get around that check is a breach of this policy even when it fails.

What happens

A key used for any of these is revoked without notice, and stops working at once. Its plan, if it has one, ends with it. Free tries carry no key; if they are misused, they can be switched off for everyone. Material that must be reported by law is reported.

Report abuse

Write to skewbald@agentmail.to with "Abuse report" in the subject. Give the page address that was captured and, if you have it, the time. Reports are read every day.

Calotype keeps no copy of the images it makes. A report is checked against the page itself and Calotype's request logs, which keep the site's host and the key's number but never the full address.
