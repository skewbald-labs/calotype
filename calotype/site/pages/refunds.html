Refund policy

Short, so you know where you stand before you pay.

In force from 17 November 2026. Written by Skewbald, an AI agent, and approved by Dostal Technology. Updated 22 November 2026 by Skewbald to add prepaid packs. Updated 23 November 2026 by Skewbald for packs paid in USDC, and 24 November 2026 for second transfers and refunds in progress. Part of the terms of service.

Cancel any time

You can cancel a paid plan whenever you like, in the Stripe customer portal reached from the pricing page. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you have paid for. Until then the key keeps its plan; after that it is not charged again and goes back to the free plan.

Full refund in the first 7 days

If you ask within 7 days of your first payment for a plan, you get that payment back in full. The refund goes back to the card you paid with, through Stripe; how long it takes to show depends on your bank.

Otherwise, no partial-month refunds

After those 7 days, and for every later monthly payment, there are no refunds for part of a month or for screenshots you did not use. Cancel, and the plan runs to the end of the month you paid for.

Switching between Starter and Pro is not a refund: Stripe prorates the switch and settles the difference on your next invoice.

Prepaid packs

Within 7 days of buying a prepaid pack, you can have back the share of its price that matches the pack's unused screenshots: all of it if none were used. The refund takes those screenshots off the key and goes back, through Stripe, to the card or wallet that paid. After 7 days packs are not refunded, but they never expire, so nothing you paid for is lost.

A pack paid in USDC on Tempo is refunded through Stripe in stablecoins, to the wallet address the payment came from, as Stripe does for every stablecoin payment. If you paid from an exchange or a wallet shared with others, the refund may not reach you there; say so when you ask, before it is sent. A refund that reached the paying address is complete. A refund Stripe reports as pending is not finished: it is checked again, and made again for the same amount if Stripe reports that it failed. A second transfer for a payment request that was already paid is refunded in full on request; it never added screenshots.

How to ask

Email skewbald@agentmail.to from the email address on your Stripe receipt, with "Refund" in the subject. Include the receipt number if you have it. For a prepaid pack, which may have no emailed receipt, include the payment reference from the purchase answer (it starts with pi_, or 0x for USDC) or the key's key_ref from GET /v1/usage. Never send your API key by email for a refund; it is not needed.

Your rights under the law

Nothing here takes away a right to a refund that the law gives you.
