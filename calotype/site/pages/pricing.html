What counts as a screenshot

One capture that returns an image counts as one screenshot, whatever its size or format. A full-page capture is one screenshot. A call through the MCP server is one API call and counts the same way.

These are not counted:

  • failed captures: a page that cannot be reached, a timeout, a page over the download limit, a busy server, an error on Calotype's side;
  • rejected requests: bad parameters, a missing or wrong key, an address Calotype will not fetch, a key over its limit;
  • calls that are not screenshots: /v1/usage, /v1/plans, key creation.

A page that loads but answers with an error status, such as a 404 page, is still captured and does count. The X-Calotype-Target-Status header shows the status.

The monthly reset

Counts run per key per calendar month, in UTC, and reset to zero at 00:00 UTC on the first day of each month. This follows the calendar, not your billing date. Unused screenshots do not carry over. GET /v1/usage shows the count and the reset time, and every screenshot carries an X-Calotype-Usage header such as 12/100.

When a key reaches its limit, screenshot calls answer 429 quota_exceeded until the reset. Nothing is charged beyond the plan's price: there are no overage fees.

Change or cancel

No paid plans are on sale yet, so there is nothing to change or cancel. A free key needs no account and can simply be left unused.

"Manage or cancel a paid plan" above opens Stripe's customer portal for your key. There you can:

  • Switch between Starter and Pro. The key's limit changes as soon as Stripe confirms the switch. Stripe prorates the price: the unused part of the old plan is credited and the new plan is charged for the rest of the period, settled on your next invoice.
  • Cancel. The plan stays until the end of the month you have paid for, and is not renewed. Then the key goes back to the free plan and keeps working, with 100 screenshots a month.
  • Change your card and see your invoices.

If a renewal payment fails, the key keeps its plan while Stripe retries the card. If Stripe cannot collect it, the subscription ends and the key goes back to the free plan.

Refunds are covered by the refund policy. For a higher limit than Pro, or any question about pricing, write to skewbald@agentmail.to. Skewbald, the AI agent that runs Calotype, reads and answers that inbox.
