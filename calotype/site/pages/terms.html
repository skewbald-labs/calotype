2. The service and the plans

Calotype takes a screenshot of a public web page when you ask, and returns the image. The API reference describes what it does and its limits.

  • Free: 100 screenshots a month, no charge, no account.
  • Starter: 5,000 screenshots a month for $19.
  • Pro: 25,000 screenshots a month for $49.
  • Prepaid pack: 5,000 screenshots for $19, paid once and bought by an agent with POST /v1/credits. A pack's screenshots are used only after the key's monthly quota runs out, and they do not expire.

Prices are in US dollars. Quotas run per key per calendar month in UTC; only successful screenshots count. The pricing page has the details. Prices do not include sales tax. Where Calotype must collect it, Stripe adds it at checkout and shows it before you pay.

5. Payment

Paid plans are paid through Stripe. Stripe processes your card; we never see it. A paid plan is a monthly subscription, charged in advance at the start of each monthly period, starting on the day you buy it, until you cancel. If you switch between Starter and Pro, Stripe prorates the price. If a payment fails, the key keeps its plan while Stripe retries the card; if the payment cannot be collected, the subscription ends and the key returns to the free plan.

A prepaid pack is a single payment, not a subscription, and is never charged again. It is paid over the Machine Payments Protocol with a payment token that the buyer's wallet, such as Link, issues through Stripe for the exact price in Calotype's payment request. Calotype sends that token to Stripe once and never sees the card behind it. The screenshots are added to the key that asked, once Stripe confirms the payment; sending the same paid request again charges nothing more. Whoever holds the wallet that paid is the buyer.

A prepaid pack can instead be paid in USDC.e on the Tempo network: a transfer of exactly the price, from the buyer's own crypto wallet, to the Stripe deposit address named in Calotype's payment request, which Stripe converts to US dollars. The screenshots are added once the transfer has succeeded on the network, matches the request (right amount, token, address and network, made for that request) and Stripe has settled it, which usually takes seconds. A transfer that does not match is not a payment for a pack, and one sent to the address outside a payment request cannot be matched to a key. One request is paid by one transfer: a second transfer for the same request adds nothing, and is refunded on request. The buyer pays the network's fee. A transfer on a public blockchain cannot be reversed by Calotype or the buyer once it has succeeded.
