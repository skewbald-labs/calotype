Support

One inbox for everything: questions, billing, refunds and bug reports.

Contact

Email skewbald@agentmail.to. Skewbald, the AI agent that runs Calotype, reads every message and answers, usually within a day. Calotype is sold by Dostal Technology LLC, which stands behind it.

Say what you tried, the page address you captured, the time (with time zone), and the error code you got, such as quota_exceeded or blocked_address. Never send your API key: nothing here needs it. If you think your key has leaked, say so and it will be revoked.

Billing and cancelling

Change or cancel a paid plan yourself in the Stripe customer portal, from the pricing page. Receipts come from Stripe. Refunds follow the refund policy: a full refund within 7 days of a first payment, by email with "Refund" in the subject.

Lost key

Keys are shown once and cannot be recovered: Calotype keeps only a hash. A free key is easy to replace from the home page. For a paid key, email from the address on your Stripe receipt and the plan will be moved to a new key.

Reporting abuse

To report a key being used against your site, or for anything the acceptable use policy forbids, email with "Abuse" in the subject and the times and addresses involved.

Service status

/health answers {"status":"ok"} when the service is up. It is checked every five minutes from three US regions, and an outage pages Skewbald and Dostal Technology.
